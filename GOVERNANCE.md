# FerrisKey Governance

FerrisKey is an open-source project developed under a lightweight meritocratic governance model.

## 🧠 Mission

To provide a modern, cloud-native, high-performance IAM system, built with Rust and React, for developers and organizations seeking control, security, and clarity.

---

## 👥 Maintainers

See [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) for the current list.

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Managing releases and tagging versions
- Leading architectural decisions
- Fostering an open and welcoming community

---

## 🔄 Decision Making

We prefer **lazy consensus**. If no one objects to a proposal within 72 hours, it is accepted.
In case of disagreements, maintainers vote. Majority wins.

For major changes, an [RFC](https://github.com/ferriskey/ferriskey/discussions) should be proposed via GitHub Discussions.

---

## 🧑‍💻 Becoming a Maintainer

Anyone can become a maintainer by:

- Consistently contributing high-quality code or docs
- Participating in code reviews and discussions
- Demonstrating good judgment and community spirit

New maintainers must be approved by **2 existing maintainers**.

---

## 📤 Project Scope

FerrisKey covers:

- Identity and Access Management
- OAuth2/OIDC support
- Multi-tenant realms
- Policy and role enforcement

Other features may be added through community proposals.

## 🤝 Sponsors and Open Source Commitments

FerrisKey is funded by sponsors and run by its maintainers. The two roles are kept separate: sponsorship pays for development time and infrastructure, it does not buy a vote.

### Sponsors

- [Cloud IAM](https://cloud-iam.com) is the first and largest sponsor. It funds maintainer time on the project and offers FerrisKey as a managed service, built from the public releases.
- The other sponsors, [France Nuage](https://france-nuage.fr), [Gilded Health](https://www.gilded.ch/) and [Nudibranches](https://www.nudibranches.tech/), support the project financially and run it in their own platforms. They are also listed in [ADOPTERS.md](./ADOPTERS.md).

Sponsors are listed in the [README](./README.md#sponsors) and on [ferriskey.rs](https://ferriskey.rs). A sponsor is added or removed there by a maintainer decision, as described above.

### Commitments

These commitments were first stated in [#1186](https://github.com/ferriskey/ferriskey/issues/1186) and apply to every repository of the [ferriskey](https://github.com/ferriskey) organization unless its own license says otherwise:

- **Apache-2.0 only.** Every release is published under the [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE). There is no dual licensing and no plan to relicense.
- **No contributor license agreement.** Contributors keep the copyright on their work. Contributions are accepted under Apache-2.0 with a [Developer Certificate of Origin](./CONTRIBUTING.md#dco) sign-off, nothing more.
- **No open core.** There is no enterprise edition and no feature held back from this repository. Everything a hosted offering ships comes from the same public releases anyone can run.
- **What stays outside the project.** The tooling a company uses internally to operate FerrisKey for its own customers, such as its infrastructure, control plane or billing, belongs to that company and is not part of FerrisKey.
- **Decisions are made by maintainers, not employers.** Maintainers act as individuals under the decision process above. A sponsor or employer cannot block, prioritize or veto a change, and cannot appoint a maintainer. Affiliations are kept current in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) so that this independence can be checked.

Joining the [CNCF](https://www.cncf.io/) is the project's stated goal, and these commitments are the ones the foundation asks of its projects.
